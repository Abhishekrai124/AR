✦ About arrai.in & Creator

A curious mind for the
digital universe.

Founded by Abhishek Rai, arrai.in is an independent digital studio and creative universe for building meaningful online experiences, interactive tools, and rich fictional worlds.

Built around intent & character.

Good digital work should feel effortless on the surface and meticulously engineered underneath. That philosophy shapes every project here—from FinTech payment architectures to deep anime lore and interactive chess engines: clear purpose, genuine personality, and details that earn their place.

The creator behind ARRAI.

Abhishek Rai is a digital creator, developer, and founder based in Ludhiana, Punjab. Connected with RaiGenZ Foundation and AR Tech Solutions, he works across web engineering, design systems, and creative writing.
