Who may enquire

Different roles.
One clear starting point.

We review requests individually. Listing a client group does not mean an existing official partnership, endorsement or guaranteed acceptance.

01 / PERSONAL

Individuals & families

Background and address enquiries, relationship concerns, person-location requests and safety-related documentation.

02 / LEGAL

Lawyers & legal teams

Authorized background research, locating people or witnesses, open-source enquiries and process-serving coordination. We do not provide legal advice or representation.

03 / PUBLIC SERVICE

Police & public authorities

Officials may submit an enquiry through an authorized channel. Any work depends on lawful authority, jurisdiction, procurement rules and written scope; this is not emergency dispatch or a substitute for official investigation.

04 / ORGANIZATIONS

Businesses & insurers

Due diligence, authorized workplace or claims enquiries, suspected fraud and relevant public-source research, subject to privacy, employment and insurance rules.

05 / COMMUNITY

Nonprofits & other organizations

Case-by-case review for legitimate locate, verification or online-research requests. We will explain limits or suggest an appropriate specialist where the request falls outside our scope.

06 / EDUCATION

Students & guardians

Students can ask about appropriate personal, online-safety or verification enquiries. If the student is under 18, involve a parent or guardian; do not share school IDs, schedules, passwords or sensitive details.
