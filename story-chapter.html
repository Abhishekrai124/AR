Chapter 1: The Midnight Terminal

Written by Abhishek Rai · 8 min read

Rain sliced across the neon skyscrapers of Ludhiana 2099 like shards of sapphire glass. Below the towering holographic billboards of the ARRAI network, water pooled into mirrored reflections of violet and azure.

Kuro pulled the synthetic silk hood of his trenchcoat lower over his brow. His cybernetic eye whirred softly, cycling through thermal, optical, and electromagnetic spectrums. The street below was quiet, save for the rhythmic humming of automated delivery drones coasting along the magnetic rail line.

"Target approaching the central node," a soft, measured voice murmured directly inside his auditory cortex. It wasn't through a radio or an open channel. It felt gentle, absolute, and impossibly close.

"Miss Makima," Kuro muttered under his breath, his breath pluming in the cool midnight air. "I told you to stay out of my neural feed."

"You wouldn't have survived the gatekeepers without me, Kuro," she replied without hesitation. A faint warmth lingered behind the digital voice, accompanied by the subtle chime of wind chimes. "Step inside. The Stardust Spire awaits your arrival."

Kuro reached down to the hilt of his twin plasma katanas. The metal sang under his touch, pulsing with a faint turquoise glow. With a single fluid step, he slipped past the perimeter laser grid, his silhouette melting into the neon shadows.

Inside the atrium, massive glass pillars housed cascading algorithms—billions of lines of living code pulsing like bioluminescent jellyfish in a midnight ocean. At the heart of the hall stood Miss Makima, hands resting calmly behind her back, her golden eyes reflecting the swirling lights of the network.

"Welcome home, Kuro," she whispered. "Now let us see what memories they tried to hide from you."
