Privacy policy

arrai.in respects your privacy. If you contact us, the information you provide—such as your name, email address and message—is used only to reply to your enquiry and manage the requested conversation. We do not sell personal information.

The site automatically counts page visits using daily aggregate totals. These new counts do not create a visitor ID, store raw IP addresses, or associate the count with a name or account. The public footer shows the all-time page-view total; the private owner dashboard shows daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, and all-time totals. These are page views, not a count of unique people. Older, previously consented analytics records may remain in private owner reports.

Location access is optional and used only when you request local weather; local time uses your device's time zone. Browser notifications are optional and require your permission. While the site is open, opted-in notifications may cover direct messages and new community posts; background notifications while the site is closed are not provided.

External platforms linked from this website, including social networks, have their own privacy policies. Please review those policies before sharing information on their services.
